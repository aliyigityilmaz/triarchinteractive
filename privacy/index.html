Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy applies to the games published by Triarch Interactive ("Triarch", "we", "us") on Steam, Google Play and Roblox, and to triarchinteractive.com. Each game's store page links here.

The short version

  • We build games, not data products. We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not ask you to create an account with us to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Progress and settings are stored on your device (or, on Roblox, in Roblox's own data storage). We do not collect them on servers of our own.
  • Our free Android games show ads from Google AdMob. They have no in-app purchases.
  • Roblox runs its own accounts, chat, payments and safety tools. We only see what Roblox makes available to every experience.

Games covered

  • PC (Steam): Keep It Contained, Keep It Checked, Tinker's Nook, Take That Shot and Endless Check.
  • Android (Google Play): Wild West Rails, Tiny Island Rescue, Idle Kingdom Island, Idle Paludarium, Merge & Rebuild, Cozy Island Farm, Dig Blast and Game Shop Keeper.
  • Roblox: every experience we publish on Roblox.

This policy also covers any future game we release that links to this page. Each platform below has its own section.

Information in all our games

Our games do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not knowingly collect personal or sensitive information through them.

  • Gameplay data on your device. Settings, progress, language and save files are stored locally on your device so the game can work. We do not receive them.
  • Platform services. Steam, Google Play and Roblox run their own services (accounts, purchases, achievements, cloud saves, crash reporting, chat). They handle that data under their own privacy policies, described in the platform sections below. We only see the aggregated or anonymous reports each platform makes available to developers.

Steam (PC games)

Keep It Contained, Keep It Checked, Tinker's Nook, Take That Shot and Endless Check are distributed through Steam. They show no ads and have no account system of ours.

  • What Steam handles. Your Steam account, profile name and avatar, purchases and payment details, achievements, Steam Cloud saves (where a game uses them), the Steam overlay and community features such as reviews and discussions. Valve processes this under the Steam Privacy Policy. Reviews and posts you publish on Steam are public.
  • What we see. Through Steamworks we see aggregated sales, wishlist and traffic figures (including country-level breakdowns) and the public reviews and posts you write. We do not receive your email address or payment details.
  • On your device. Settings and save files are kept in your game's local save folder, or in Steam Cloud if the game uses it. Uninstalling the game or deleting the save folder removes the local copy.

Google Play (Android games)

Wild West Rails, Tiny Island Rescue, Idle Kingdom Island, Idle Paludarium, Merge & Rebuild, Cozy Island Farm, Dig Blast and Game Shop Keeper are distributed through Google Play. They are free to play and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google Play itself (downloads, updates, payments, ratings and reviews) runs under the Google Privacy Policy. Through the Play Console we see aggregated install, country, device and crash statistics, and the public reviews you write.

Advertising

The games show these kinds of ads:

  • Wild West Rails: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run, and an interstitial ad that can appear between runs. Ads never appear while you are building or driving trains.
  • Tiny Island Rescue: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch.
  • Idle Kingdom Island: optional rewarded video ads that you watch in exchange for a bonus, and an occasional short full-screen ad after a kingdom level-up.
  • Merge & Rebuild: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between levels.
  • Idle Paludarium, Cozy Island Farm, Dig Blast and Game Shop Keeper: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads.

Rewarded ads are always optional.

What the ad SDK collects. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in these games may collect and share with Google: your IP address (used to estimate a general location), your Android advertising ID and app set ID, device and operating-system information (such as model, OS version, language, app version and network type), product interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information (such as launch time and crash or hang data). This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive it and we do not build profiles of players. Google acts as an independent provider; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosures.

Consent and your ad choices

  • Consent form. When you first open one of these games, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's Settings screen.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics and crash reports (Wild West Rails only)

Wild West Rails also uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. They collect anonymous usage events (for example which map was played, how long a run lasted, whether a tutorial step was finished), device and app information (device model, Android version, language, app version), crash and error reports, and a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. They do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location, and we do not combine this data with any other data. Where the law requires a consent choice, collection starts only after you have made it and stops if you withdraw it. Google processes this data under its own terms: Firebase privacy information. Our other Android games do not use Firebase and have no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of their own.

Notifications (Wild West Rails)

If you switch on the daily reminder in Settings, the game schedules at most one local notification a day on your device. It does not use a server and you can switch it off at any time.

What stays on your device

Settings, progress, best scores and timestamps used to calculate offline earnings are stored only in each game's private storage on your device. These games have no account system, no server of ours and no in-app purchases. Uninstalling a game, clearing its storage or using the in-game reset option removes that data.

Roblox experiences

This section applies to every experience we publish on Roblox, now and in the future. Roblox is operated by Roblox Corporation, not by us. You need a Roblox account to play, and Roblox, not Triarch, handles sign-up, age settings, chat, parental controls and payments. Whenever you play on Roblox, the Roblox Terms of Use and the Roblox Privacy and Cookie Policy apply.

  • What we receive. As the creator of an experience, we receive the information Roblox makes available to creators: your username, display name and user ID, game metrics (such as how long and how often an experience is played), details of purchases made in our experience, and your regional location. Roblox bases the location on your IP address but does not give us the IP address itself.
  • How we use it. To run the experience, save your progress, deliver items you have bought or earned, understand how the experience is played so we can improve it, and prevent cheating and abuse.
  • Saved progress. Progress such as currency, items and settings is saved in Roblox's own data storage (DataStores) against your user ID. It is kept on Roblox's infrastructure, and we do not copy it to servers of our own.
  • What we do not collect. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, address or photos, and we do not record voice, video or chat. Chat in our experiences runs through Roblox's own filtered chat system. Our experiences do not show third-party ads and do not use third-party analytics.
  • Purchases. If one of our experiences offers items, passes or other extras for Robux, Roblox processes the payment, and we only see that a purchase was made for a user ID so that we can deliver it. We never see payment details. Refunds are handled by Roblox under its own terms.
  • Deleting your data. Email us at triarchinteractive@gmail.com with your Roblox username and user ID, and we will delete the saved game data we hold for your account and confirm by email. Deleting it resets your progress. To delete your Roblox account or the information Roblox holds, contact Roblox directly.
  • Reporting problems. Use Roblox's in-experience report tools to report abuse or inappropriate behaviour. Roblox moderates its platform.

Roblox is open to players of all ages, so children may play our experiences. Roblox applies age-based safety settings and offers parental controls that parents manage in their child's Roblox account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what Roblox makes available to every experience.

Information on our website

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate region, device and browser type). Google may set cookies or similar identifiers for this. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.
  • Theme preference. If you pick a light or dark theme, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address to serve pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above (Google for website analytics, ads and, in Wild West Rails, game analytics and crash reports; GitHub for hosting; and Valve (Steam), Google Play and Roblox, which distribute our games) and when the law requires it. We do not sell your information or share it for third-party marketing.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Roblox save data stays until you ask us to delete it or the experience is retired. Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you. Data held by Google, Firebase, Valve, Roblox or GitHub follows their own retention policies, and we cannot look it up or delete it for you.

Children

Our Steam games, Android games and website are not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country, for example 16 in parts of the EU), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad-supported Android games are not designed for children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Roblox is open to players of all ages; see the Roblox section above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, the KVKK in Türkiye, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data we hold about you, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Because we hold very little, most requests are simple and many are covered by the controls described above. Email us and we will respond. Requests about advertising data collected by Google, or data held by Valve or Roblox, are best sent to them using the links above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security and international transfers

Data stored on your device is protected by your operating system's app sandbox. Google, Valve, Roblox and GitHub may process data in other countries under their own safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, or if a game starts to collect more or different data (for example analytics or cloud save), we will post the new version here and update the date above before the change is released.
