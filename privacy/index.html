Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy applies to the games published by Triarch Interactive ("Triarch", "we", "us") and to triarchinteractive.com. It covers Keep It Contained, Keep It Checked, Tinker's Nook, Take That Shot, Endless Check, Wild West Rails, Tiny Island Rescue, Idle Kingdom Island, and any future game we release that links to this page.

Our approach

We build games, not data products. We do not sell personal data, and we do not ask you to create an account with us to play. Most of our games show no ads. Three free Android games, Wild West Rails, Tiny Island Rescue and Idle Kingdom Island, show ads from Google AdMob, as described in the "Advertising in our free Android games" section below.

Information in our games

Our games do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not knowingly collect personal or sensitive information through them.

  • Gameplay data on your device. Settings, progress and save files are stored locally on your device so the game can work. We do not receive them.
  • Platform services. Games distributed through Steam or another store use that platform's own services (for example purchases, achievements, cloud saves and crash reporting). The platform handles that data under its own privacy policy, such as the Steam Privacy Policy. We only see aggregated or anonymous reports the platform makes available to developers.

Advertising in our free Android games

Wild West Rails, Tiny Island Rescue and Idle Kingdom Island are free to play and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Our other games are not affected by this section unless their own store page says they show ads. The games show these kinds of ads:

  • Wild West Rails: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run, and an interstitial ad that can appear between runs. Ads never appear while you are building or driving trains.
  • Tiny Island Rescue: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch.
  • Idle Kingdom Island: optional rewarded video ads that you watch in exchange for a bonus, and an occasional short full-screen ad after a kingdom level-up.

What the ad SDK collects. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in these games may collect and share with Google: your IP address (used to estimate a general location), your Android advertising ID and app set ID, device and operating-system information, product interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information (such as launch time and crash or hang data). This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive it and we do not build profiles of players. Google acts as an independent provider; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosures.

Analytics and crash reports (Wild West Rails only). Wild West Rails also uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. They collect anonymous usage events (for example which map was played, how long a run lasted, whether a tutorial step was finished), device and app information (device model, Android version, language, app version), crash and error reports, and a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. They do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location, and we do not combine this data with any other data. Where the law requires a consent choice, collection starts only after you have made it and stops if you withdraw it. Google processes this data under its own terms: Firebase privacy information. Tiny Island Rescue and Idle Kingdom Island do not use Firebase and have no analytics of their own.

Notifications (Wild West Rails). If you switch on the daily reminder in Settings, the game schedules at most one local notification a day on your device. It does not use a server and you can switch it off at any time.

Your choices.

  • Consent form. When you first open one of these games, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy settings button in the game's Settings screen.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What stays on your device. Settings, progress, best scores and timestamps used to calculate offline earnings are stored only on your device. These games have no account system, no server of ours and no in-app purchases. Uninstalling a game or clearing its storage removes that data.

Information on our website

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate region, device and browser type). Google may set cookies or similar identifiers for this. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.
  • Theme preference. If you pick a light or dark theme, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address to serve pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above (Google for website analytics, ads and, in Wild West Rails, game analytics and crash reports; GitHub for hosting; and the stores that distribute our games) and when the law requires it. We do not sell your information.

Children

Our games and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad-supported games listed above are not designed for children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to its processing. Because we hold very little, most requests are simple. Email us and we will respond. Requests about advertising data collected by Google are best sent to Google using the links above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
