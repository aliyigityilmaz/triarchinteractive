Description

Keep It Contained is a cooperative, communication-driven game set inside a sealed, high-risk research facility. One player is the scientist inside, working the panels, devices and organism modules. The others form the external control team, and all they have is the Protocol Book.

The player inside cannot see the protocols. The team outside cannot see the modules or the organism. The only bridge is talking: describing a symbol accurately, calling steps in the right order, staying calm when the clock is running. One misdescribed symbol or skipped step and the organism gets out.

Sessions are short and intense. System failures, surprise events and organism-specific behaviour keep everyone on edge. Silence is dangerous — but so is talking too much.

Features

  • Asymmetric co-op — one player inside the facility, everyone else on the protocols.
  • One copy is enough — only the player inside needs the game; the Protocol Book is free on the official site, printable or on any device.
  • Dynamic scenarios — procedurally generated module layouts and randomised organism traits.
  • Progressive difficulty — new modules, new organisms and more pressure as you advance.
  • Level editor — build your own missions, module combinations and difficulty settings.
  • Play anywhere together — same room, or remote with Discord or any voice chat (Steam Remote Play Together supported).
