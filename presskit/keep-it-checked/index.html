Description

You are a researcher in a facility that studies anomalous phenomena. One day you leave your office and something goes wrong: the corridor ahead looks familiar, the facility looks unchanged, and every path leads back to where you have already been.

To get out you have to trust your eyes. A missing object, an extra one, a change in the room, something that simply shouldn't be there. If you see an anomaly, turn back. If everything is normal, keep going. Get it right enough times in a row and the loop opens.

Some anomalies are obvious. Others take real attention. The facility studies anomalies — today, you are studying it.

Features

  • Observation-based anomaly hunting — the whole game is noticing what changed.
  • Simple rules, hard calls — anomaly: go back. No anomaly: go forward.
  • Randomised anomalies — every run shuffles what appears and where.
  • Liminal research-facility atmosphere — quiet corridors that slowly stop feeling safe.
  • Full controller support — including Steam Deck.
  • 13 languages — at launch.
